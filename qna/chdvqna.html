COALITION FOR HOMELESS AND DISABLED VETERANS PAC

Source: CHDV PAC SCRIPT.docx

QUESTION: IF A QUESTION IS NOT ON HERE?
RESPONSE: PLEASE REFER THE CONSUMER TO THE CORRESPONDING "800" NUMBER: GET ONE

QUESTION: WHAT IS THE COALITION FOR HOMELESS AND DISABLED VETERANS PAC?
RESPONSE: THE COALITION FOR HOMELESS AND DISABLED VETERANS PAC IS AN INITIATIVE OF NATIONAL COALITION FOR DISABLED VETERANS PAC. OUR HOPE IS TO RAISE AWARENESS ABOUT VETERANS LEGISLATION AND TO ENCOURAGE PEOPLE TO TAKE ACTION BY ELECTING A CANDIDATE THAT WILL HELP SUPPORT HOMELESS VETERANS AND VETERANS STRUGGLING WITH MENTAL HEALTH ISSUES.

QUESTION: ARE YOU A MEMBER OF THE COALITION FOR HOMELESS AND DISABLED VETERANS PAC?
RESPONSE: NO, I WORK FOR A PROFESSIONAL FUNDRAISING COMPANY WHICH HAS BEEN CONTRACTED BY THE COALITION FOR HOMELESS AND DISABLED VETERANS PAC TO HELP.

QUESTION: IS THIS A DEMOCRAT OR REPUBLICAN ORGANIZATION?
RESPONSE: THIS ORGANIZATION IS NOT DEMOCRATIC OR REPUBLICAN; IT IS A NON PARTISAN POLITICAL ORGANIZATION FOCUSING ON THE NEEDS OF OUR VETERANS.

QUESTION: WHO IS THE HEAD OF THIS ORGANIZATION?
RESPONSE: THIS POLITICAL ORGANIZATION WAS ORGANIZED AND IS MANAGED BY A GROUP OF CITIZENS CONCERNED WITH THE NUMBER OF VETERANS IN CONGRESS.

QUESTION: WHAT IS THE TAX STATUS OF THIS ORGANIZATION?
RESPONSE: CONTRIBUTIONS AND DONATIONS TO THE COALITION FOR HOMELESS AND DISABLED VETERANS PAC ARE NOT CHARITABLE CONTRIBUTIONS AND ARE NOT DEDUCTIBLE AS CHARITABLE CONTRIBUTIONS FOR STATE AND FEDERAL INCOME TAX PURPOSES.

QUESTION: WHERE IS THE COALITION FOR HOMELESS AND DISABLED VETERANS PAC?
RESPONSE: THE COALITION FOR HOMELESS AND DISABLED VETERANS PAC IS HEADQUARTERED IN CHICAGO, IL.

QUESTION: HOW MUCH GOES TO THE COALITION FOR HOMELESS AND DISABLED VETERANS PAC?
RESPONSE: THERE IS NO SET PERCENTAGE. THERE ARE SUBSTANTIAL FUNDRAISING EXPENSES, PROCESSING FEES, AND NORMAL FEC COMPLIANCE EXPENSES. BUT THE PLAN IS TO HAVE ENOUGH FUNDS LEFT OVER TO SUPPORT THE CANDIDATES WHO WILL TRULY HELP THE CAUSE.

QUESTION: DO THEY HAVE A WEBSITE ADDRESS I CAN VISIT?
RESPONSE: YOU CAN VISIT THE COALITION FOR HOMELESS AND DISABLED VETERANS PAC ON THE WEB AT WWW.DISABLEDVETS.ORG

QUESTION: DOES THIS HELP LOCALLY?
RESPONSE: THIS IS A NATIONAL PROGRAM.

QUESTION: WHO DO YOU WORK FOR?
RESPONSE: I WORK FOR A PROFESSIONAL FUNDRAISING COMPANY HIRED BY THE PAC TO CONDUCT THIS DONOR AWARENESS APPEAL.
